1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Namaz Alarm ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring transparency regarding the device permissions requested and data handled by the application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Namaz Alarm functions as an offline-first prayer companion. The app does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identification.

A. Location Information

What we access: Precise and approximate location (GPS / Network location via ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
Why we need it: Accurate calculation of Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Tahajjud, Ishraq), astronomical sun angles, and Qibla direction depends strictly on geographic coordinates.
How it is handled: Your location is computed locally on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers, stored remotely, or shared with third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

B. Alarms, Schedules & Audio

What we access: Exact alarm scheduling (USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), device wake-lock (WAKE_LOCK), device vibration (VIBRATE), full-screen intents (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT), and foreground audio service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK).
Why we need it: To ensure that your configured prayer alarms ring at the exact minute requested, display full-screen alarm alerts on the lock screen, and play your chosen Azan or ringtone even when your device is locked or in battery-saver/doze mode.

C. Local Data Storage

The app stores your custom alarm preferences, prayer time offsets, snooze settings, and notification choices locally on your device using an internal SQLite/Room database. This data never leaves your device.
